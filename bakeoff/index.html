Run the bake-off to fill this table. Rate answers below to add your own quality score; it's the most important column.
What the columns mean
  • Load: seconds to download (first time) or load from cache, and compile on the GPU.
  • Sec/thought: average time per character answer. Lower means characters can think more often.
  • Tok/s: approximate output speed.
  • Valid JSON: answers the game could read.
  • Rules OK: answers that picked an allowed action and filled every field.
  • Distinct: how different the five characters sound from each other (100 = no shared wording). Low scores mean persona collapse.
  • Your rating: average of the stars you give. Judge: does this sound like this character, and is the decision sensible?
